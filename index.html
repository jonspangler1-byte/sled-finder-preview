Sled Finder

A membership app for antique sled collectors, from the Rosebud Sled Museum in Schaumburg, Illinois. Browse makers and models, keep a record of the sleds you own, and share finds with other collectors.

Maker names, places and dates come from Jonathan Spangler's 61-company matrix; histories come from the Rosebud company briefs.
